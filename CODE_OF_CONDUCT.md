# Contributor Covenant and community expectations

## Our commitment
The Anime Book Store project aims to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for contributors and users. Participation is expected to be professional and considerate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or role.

## Expected behavior
- Use welcoming and respectful language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on code, decisions, and behavior rather than people.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant and assume good faith while addressing impact.
- Protect project data, credentials, and private communications.
- Credit contributions accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and misuse of stored catalogue or user data are not acceptable. Deliberate disruption of project infrastructure or attempts to expose secrets are also prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibilities
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and explained where practical.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through [GitHub @Brajesh31](https://github.com/Brajesh31). Do not publish personal information or sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.

## Scope
These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, code review, project discussions, and project-related communication spaces.

## Acknowledgment
By participating in this repository, contributors agree to follow these community expectations.
